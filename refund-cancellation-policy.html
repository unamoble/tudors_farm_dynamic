Refund & Cancellation Policy for Tudor Farm Stay

This policy explains how cancellations, booking modifications, and no-show refunds are handled for reservations made with Tudor Farm Stay.

Last updated: [Month Day, Year]

1. Scope

This policy applies to direct room bookings made with Tudor Farm Stay, unless a separate written agreement or specific booking offer states otherwise.

2. Key Booking Terms

To avoid any confusion, we use the following terms in this policy:

  • Cancellation: A guest informs us before the scheduled check-in that they will not use the booking.
  • Rescheduling: A guest requests a change of stay dates before check-in, subject to availability and applicable terms.
  • No-show: A guest does not arrive within the period described in Section 4 below.

3. Cancellations and Modifications Before Check-In

If you need to cancel or modify your booking before the scheduled check-in time, please contact Tudor Farm Stay as early as possible.

Cancellation and modification requests made before check-in are handled according to the applicable booking/cancellation terms in effect for your reservation.

Rescheduling requests are subject to room availability, rate differences, and any applicable booking conditions.

4. Check-In Delay and No-Show Rule

If a guest is unable to arrive at the scheduled check-in time, the reserved room will be held exclusively for that guest for up to 24 hours from the scheduled check-in time.

If the guest does not arrive within this 24-hour period, the booking will be treated as a no-show.

5. No-Show Refund Amount

In case of a no-show under Section 4, 50% of the amount actually paid for the booking will be refunded.

The remaining 50% may be retained to compensate for the room being held and the lost opportunity to accommodate another guest during that period.

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed through the original payment method where possible, or by another mutually agreed method.

Processing times may vary depending on payment channel, banking systems, and intermediary providers.

7. Guest Communication

If you anticipate a delay or inability to arrive, please notify Tudor Farm Stay as soon as possible so we can support you with the best available options.
